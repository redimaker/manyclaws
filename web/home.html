What we can’t read

Sealed before it leaves your desk.

Your sessions are sealed with a key made from a passphrase you choose. Each of your devices makes the key itself, from the passphrase you type there, and nothing of either is ever sent to us: no key, no hash, not even whether you have a passphrase. Each computer seals what its sessions say before sending it: prompts, replies, tool calls and their output, photos, titles, even the text of your alerts. Your phone opens it with the same key. There is no setting for this, and no way round it: we refuse anything that arrives unsealed.

We can see
that a session is running, on which of your computers, and when.
We can’t see
what you asked, what Claude said, or what it ran.
We never have
your password or your passphrase. Your own devices turn them into what is needed; neither leaves them.
The catch
a forgotten password is set again by a code to your email. A forgotten passphrase is not: we never had it, and what was sealed stays sealed.

Asked, mostly before paying

Does Claude run on your servers?

No. Claude Code runs on your own computers, signed in to your own Claude account, as it does today. ManyClaws is the part in between: it carries what your sessions say to your phone, and your answers back.

Do I need a particular Claude plan?

Whatever plan Claude Code is signed in to works. ManyClaws doesn’t use your Claude allowance for anything of its own.

Will this keep working if I switch accounts?

Yes. ManyClaws follows your sessions, whichever Claude account they’re signed in to. Switch accounts in the middle of a session and it stays where it was on your page. Different computers can be signed in to different accounts, and they all show up together.

Is this made by Anthropic?

No. ManyClaws is an independent product that works with Claude Code through its plugin system. It isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.

What if my phone is off?

Nothing breaks. Every prompt is still asked in the terminal as well, and the first answer wins. You are adding a second place to say yes, not moving the only one.

How do the alerts get to an iPhone?

Add the page to your Home Screen from Safari’s Share menu, open it from there, and turn on alerts. On Android and on a desk, the browser asks you once.

Do you store any data?

Yes: your sessions’ chats, for fourteen days, and only encrypted. We keep them for two reasons. Most connections upload slowly, so your phone gets a chat from us much faster than from your computer. And a computer that is asleep or restarting can’t answer at all, while its chats can still be read here. All of it is sealed on your computer before it is sent, and only your own devices can open it, with the passphrase you enter on each of them. We can’t read any of it. A chat is removed fourteen days after we last hear from its session, and you can clear all of them from your account page whenever you like: your computers send them again as they connect. The transcripts on your own computers are yours and stay where they are.
